How to Chant Correctly

The Gayatri Mantra is most effective when chanted during the ambrosial hours (Sandhya) - dawn, noon, and dusk. Follow these steps for proper chanting:

  1. Sit comfortably in a clean, quiet place facing east
  2. Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths
  3. Chant maintaining proper pronunciation and rhythm
  4. Visualize divine light illuminating your mind
  5. Repeat at least 108 times using a mala (prayer beads)

May the Gayatri Mantra illuminate your path to enlightenment
