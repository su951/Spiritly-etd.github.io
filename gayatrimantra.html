ॐ गायत्री मन्त्र

The Supreme Vedic Mantra for Enlightenment

The Gayatri Mantra

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं
भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥
Om Bhur Bhuvaḥ Swaḥ
Tat Savitur Vareṇyaṃ
Bhargo Devasya Dhīmahi
Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Prachodayāt
We meditate upon the divine effulgence of that adorable Sun of spiritual consciousness;
May it enlighten our intellect and guide us on the path of righteousness.

Significance of Gayatri Mantra

The Gayatri Mantra is considered one of the most universal of all Hindu mantras, invoking the universal Brahman as the principle of knowledge and the illumination of the primordial Sun.
